# Video Style Guide

## Creative Direction

Keep the video product-led, warm, and calm: a cream canvas, forest-green accents, restrained type, and one centered phone. The app already supplies a distinctive organic background and mascot; the edit should not add generic fintech gradients, floating coins, holograms, or AI imagery.

Use the actual `Logo_Mark.png` and `Wordmark_Primary.png`. The approved in-app tagline is `TRACK SMARTER. LIVE BETTER.`

## Canvas and Device Framing

### Vertical 9:16

- Master: 1080 × 1920, portrait.
- Capture: native device resolution at 60 fps when possible; minimum 1080 px wide.
- Place the phone/UI at roughly 88–94% of canvas height, centered.
- If the capture already includes full-screen device UI, let it fill the frame; do not add a second device bezel.
- Background: `#F5F2E8`, optionally with one very faint organic corner asset at under 12% opacity.
- Keep captions inside a 120 px top/bottom safe margin and away from the app's bottom navigation.

### Presentation 16:9

- Master: 1920 × 1080.
- Preserve the portrait capture's aspect ratio; never stretch it.
- Place the phone at 560–650 px high, slightly right of center (about 62% canvas width).
- Use the left third for the scene caption or a short benefit line.
- Keep the phone at least 90 px from every canvas edge.
- A subtle contact shadow is enough: deep-forest black at roughly 12% opacity, 0/18 px offset, 40 px blur.

## Typography and Captions

- Use Plus Jakarta Sans to match the app.
- Titles: ExtraBold; captions: SemiBold; supporting labels: Regular/Medium.
- Vertical captions: approximately 44–54 px at 1080 × 1920.
- Presentation captions: approximately 36–44 px at 1920 × 1080.
- Use deep forest on a cream pill, or cream on a deep-forest pill.
- Maximum 3–7 words; one line when possible, two lines maximum.
- Fade in/out over 120–180ms. Avoid kinetic word-by-word animation.

## Camera Treatment

Full-device, stable framing is the default. Four emphasis moves are enough for the full video:

1. 100% → 106% on the opening summary.
2. 100% → 108% on the scanner frame.
3. 100% → 112% on receipt review, then return to 100% before save.
4. 100% → 108% on analytics.

Use the separate camera plan for shot timing. Never stack a zoom over an app navigation transition. No scale should exceed 112% in the recommended cut.

## Transitions

- Use native UI transitions whenever navigation is visible.
- Use straight cuts between source takes that represent continuous UI state.
- One short cross dissolve is acceptable before the ending.
- Use a match cut from the photographed receipt to the processing screen if composition allows.
- Do not use spins, whip pans, glitches, fake depth, or aggressive motion blur.

## Pacing

- Opening: 1 second to identify, 4 seconds to understand the dashboard.
- Action scenes: 4–7 seconds.
- Receipt review: up to 11 seconds because it carries the trust story.
- Result screens: 4–6 seconds each.
- Ending: 3 seconds.
- Let a UI state settle for at least 350ms before cutting.

## Audio

- Style: minimal technology / soft electronic / modern productivity / subtle fintech.
- Tempo: 92–108 BPM.
- Mood: warm, precise, optimistic, low-drama.
- Arrangement: light pulse, muted pluck, soft pad; no vocals.
- Keep music about 14–18 dB below narration; duck a further 2–3 dB under dense review narration.
- Use subtle UI sounds only for Scan-sheet open, camera capture, confirm/save, and analytics reveal.
- UI sounds should be original or licensed, quiet, and under 150ms except the camera shutter.
- Do not add a synthetic OCR/AI voice or futuristic scan sound that implies functionality not present.

## Opening Frame

Start directly on the live Home Dashboard. Fade the real ExpenSense wordmark in at the top safe area for about one second, then remove it so the UI remains primary. Do not replay the full 3.2-second app splash.

## Ending Frame

Return to the updated Home Dashboard. Ease back to full-device view while the real wordmark and `TRACK SMARTER. LIVE BETTER.` fade in. Hold for 1.5–2 seconds, then fade to `#F5F2E8`.

## Export

- Capture frame rate: 60 fps preferred, 30 fps acceptable if device performance is more stable.
- Edit timeline: 60 fps for smooth native motion; deliver an additional 30 fps copy if required by the platform.
- Codec: H.264 High Profile in MP4; H.265 optional for archive/delivery where compatible.
- Vertical bitrate: 16–24 Mbps for the master, 10–16 Mbps for social upload.
- Presentation bitrate: 18–30 Mbps for the master.
- Audio: AAC, 48 kHz, 320 kbps stereo.
- Color: Rec.709 SDR. Avoid automatic HDR capture unless the entire pipeline is color managed.
- Final duration: 58 seconds; hard maximum 60 seconds.
