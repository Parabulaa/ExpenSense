# Voice-over

ExpenSense brings everyday spending, budgets, and insights into one clear view.

Add an expense manually, upload a photo, or scan the receipt in the moment. Frame the full receipt, then capture it with one tap.

ExpenSense checks image quality, reads the receipt, and verifies its structure and totals. Before anything is saved, review the merchant, items, category, wallet, and totals—and see exactly what will change.

The purchase becomes a categorized transaction in the ledger, with its receipt source preserved. Its wallet balance and category budget update from the same confirmed record.

Analytics reveal where spending is concentrated, while insights surface recurring merchants and budget pressure.

So every receipt becomes a clearer picture of your finances.

Word count: 115. Read at roughly 120–125 words per minute, with short pauses at the processing, save, and analytics transitions.
